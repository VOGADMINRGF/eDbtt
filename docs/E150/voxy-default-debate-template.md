# Voxy Default Debate Template / eDebatte Visual SSOT

Status: 2026-06-26 canonical default template

## Purpose

This document defines the default visual and editorial template for future eDebatte / VoiceOpenGov / Voxy debate outputs.

It is based on the validated Voxy debate-poster pattern developed in the 2026-06-26 working session and must be treated as the default reference for new debate-style social, poster, carousel and studio outputs unless a dossier explicitly requires a different format.

## Template Thesis

Voxy is not a decorative mascot. Voxy is the debate host: a recognizable moderator figure that asks the central public question, structures the conflict, keeps both sides visible and translates observations into a concrete reform or governance logic.

Default framing:

- one strong public question
- one clear thesis underneath
- visible everyday pain points / observed patterns
- fair structural diagnosis
- obligations for both sides
- concrete reform, rule or governance proposal
- review-safe language, no personal attack
- final civic call-to-action

## Default Visual DNA

Use this as the default direction for generated or designed eDebatte visuals:

- dark VOG/eDebatte studio look
- neon blue accent system
- Voxy centered or left-centered as host / presenter
- podcast, debate studio, microphone or On Air visual cues
- large headline question in the upper left or upper center
- concise thesis directly under the headline
- right-side evidence / observation column titled similar to `Was viele erleben:`
- middle or lower section titled similar to `Zeit für eine Reform. Für beide Seiten.`
- bottom governance / reform block with clear rule logic
- VOG / eDebatte branding, `Every voice. Every time.` and/or `Deine Stimme. Unsere Zukunft.`

The look should feel premium, civic, debate-ready and shareable: less meme, more public issue briefing with personality.

## Default Layout Contract

Recommended single-poster layout:

1. **Header / Studio frame**
   - `VOXY fragt:`
   - large question, e.g. `Arbeitsmarkt oder Theater?`
   - optional `ON AIR` marker

2. **Core thesis**
   - short, quotable line
   - e.g. `Ein starker Case macht noch keinen Erfolg. Und eine offene Stelle noch keinen echten Einstellungsprozess.`

3. **Voxy host block**
   - Voxy visible as central narrator
   - microphone / headset / studio context
   - optional speech bubble: `Wen prüft hier eigentlich wen?`

4. **Observation column**
   - titled `Was viele erleben:` or topic-specific equivalent
   - 4-6 concise cards with icons
   - each card describes a structural pattern, not a personal grievance

5. **Fairness / both-sides block**
   - title: `Zeit für eine Reform. Für beide Seiten.`
   - left column: what institutions / companies / authorities should change
   - right column: what citizens / applicants / employees / participants must also uphold
   - principle: fairness is not a one-way street

6. **Governance / reform block**
   - titled with a concrete reform claim
   - includes scope boundaries, safeguards, misuse protection and review gates
   - no vague outrage; always end with rule logic

7. **Footer / CTA**
   - VOG + eDebatte brand lockup
   - `Every voice. Every time.`
   - `Deine Stimme. Unsere Zukunft.`
   - optional QR / participation link placeholder

## Editorial Tone

Default tone:

- pointed, humorous and easy to understand
- systemic rather than personally bitter
- critical but fair
- politically and legally careful
- clear about uncertainty and reform boundaries
- no unverified allegations against named people or organizations
- no founder/personality framing as the center of the output

The ideal Voxy voice is: sharp enough to create attention, fair enough to invite debate, structured enough to become a civic product.

## Co-Creation Workflow with Potential Authors

A Voxy/eDebatte output must not be produced as a one-shot prompt from a vague complaint. The default workflow is a guided co-creation process with the potential author, initiator or topic owner.

Goal: turn lived observation, frustration or expertise into a public-interest debate output without losing the author's intent, over-personalizing the topic or creating unsupported claims.

### Required Co-Creation Stages

1. **Author intent capture**
   - capture the author's raw observation in their own words
   - identify what they are really trying to say
   - separate emotion, anecdote, evidence, suspicion and reform wish
   - preserve the author's core thesis before polishing language

2. **Issue reframing**
   - translate the personal observation into a structural public question
   - avoid making the author the protagonist unless explicitly intended
   - ask: `What is the public-interest issue behind this experience?`
   - define the target frame: workplace, education, administration, housing, health, mobility, democracy, etc.

3. **Evidence and boundary check**
   - mark what is verified, experienced, assumed or open
   - remove or soften unsupported accusations
   - keep sensitive allegations generic unless a reviewed dossier contains verified evidence
   - decide whether legal, HR, medical, political or reputational review is required

4. **Both-sides fairness pass**
   - identify what side A must change
   - identify what side B must also uphold
   - prevent one-sided outrage framing
   - make responsibilities, incentives and consequences visible

5. **Reform logic extraction**
   - convert the issue into one concrete rule, reform, governance mechanism or process improvement
   - include safeguards against misuse
   - define what should be allowed, required, prohibited or reviewed
   - make the proposal specific enough to be debated

6. **Voxy translation**
   - convert the topic into the default Voxy structure:
     - `VOXY fragt: [public question]`
     - core thesis
     - observed patterns
     - both-sides obligations
     - reform / governance block
     - civic CTA
   - keep the author's sharpness, but make it publication-safe

7. **Author confirmation gate**
   - before final rendering, confirm:
     - `Does this still say what you wanted to say?`
     - `Is anything too personal, too soft or too risky?`
     - `Are the obligations for both sides fair?`
     - `Is the reform proposal concrete enough?`
   - no final output should be treated as author-approved before this gate

8. **Editorial review gate**
   - check tone, source status, legal/reputational risk and visual consistency
   - ensure the result is review-gated and dossier-bound before publication or export

### Co-Creation Intake Questions

When a topic starts from a potential author, Voxy should guide the author with a small set of questions before drafting:

- `Was ist der konkrete Auslöser?`
- `Welche strukturelle Frage steckt dahinter?`
- `Welche Aussage muss unbedingt erhalten bleiben?`
- `Was darf auf keinen Fall wie ein persönlicher Angriff wirken?`
- `Welche Seite wird oft vergessen?`
- `Was wäre eine faire Regel oder Reform?`
- `Welche Beispiele sind erlebt, welche belegt, welche nur Vermutung?`
- `Soll der Ton eher humorvoll, scharf, sachlich oder reformorientiert sein?`

### Access / Entitlement Framing

The Voxy default debate template must be compatible with staged access gates, but those gates apply only to studio work, premium workup and partner/operator workflows, not to the public readability of already published public debates.

Contract framing:

- public topic intake may stay low-friction
- authenticated members may use light Voxy intake
- full co-creation, visual brief generation and export prep are product-gated studio capabilities
- partner and operator scopes may prepare campaigns or editorial review workflows
- published public debates must not be hidden behind a hard paywall in this slice

Guardrails:

- no payment or checkout provider is implied by these gates
- no auto-publish follows from access to review or export preparation
- no auto-dossier or auto-Anlassraum creation follows from co-creation access
- author confirmation and editorial review remain separate required gates

### Author Collaboration Contract

For product and UX, the co-creation state should be treated as its own pre-output object, not as final copy.

Minimum fields:

- `authorIntent`
- `rawObservation`
- `nonNegotiableThesis`
- `structuralIssue`
- `publicQuestion`
- `verifiedFacts`
- `assumptions`
- `openQuestions`
- `sensitiveClaims`
- `bothSidesObligations`
- `reformProposal`
- `safeguards`
- `tonePreference`
- `authorApprovalStatus`
- `editorialReviewStatus`

Required status enums:

- `authorApprovalStatus`: `draft`, `needs_author_confirmation`, `confirmed`, `rejected`
- `editorialReviewStatus`: `not_submitted`, `needs_review`, `in_review`, `changes_requested`, `approved_for_export`, `rejected`

Review semantics:

- `approved_for_export` is an export-preparation state, not a publication state
- publication remains separately review-bound and must not be inferred from co-creation status alone

### Acceptance Criteria for Co-Creation

A topic is ready for Voxy visual/output generation only when:

- the author's core thesis is preserved
- the issue has been reframed as a public-interest question
- unsupported claims are marked, softened or removed
- both sides are represented fairly
- the reform/governance proposal is concrete enough to debate
- the author has confirmed the direction or the output is clearly marked as unapproved draft
- editorial review is still required before publication

## Mandatory Guardrails

Every Voxy debate output must respect the existing Output Engine / Studio guardrails:

- Dossier remains source of truth.
- Outputs are review-required before publication.
- No auto-publish behavior.
- No auto-dossier behavior.
- No auto-Anlassraum behavior.
- No fake external channel integration.
- Open questions, source gaps and uncertainty remain visible.
- No legal advisory claim.
- No hidden persuasion or astroturfing.
- No personal attacks, defamation or unsupported accusations.

For sensitive employment, governance, health, legal or political topics, copy must be framed as a debate question or reform proposal unless the underlying dossier contains verified evidence.

## Example Pattern: Arbeitsmarkt / Recruiting

This topic pattern is the current reference example:

Main question:

`VOXY fragt: Arbeitsmarkt oder Theater?`

Core thesis:

`Ein starker Case macht noch keinen Erfolg. Und eine offene Stelle noch keinen echten Einstellungsprozess.`

Observation cards:

- `Endlose Prozesse: 5-8 Runden, verschiedene Stakeholder, lange Wartezeiten, keine klare Entscheidung.`
- `Gratis-Beratung statt Respekt: Marktanalyse, Strategie, Konzepte und Pläne - oft ohne Vergütung und ohne ehrliche Rückmeldung.`
- `Schein-Vakanzen? Monate- oder jahrelang ausgeschrieben, aber intern nicht wirklich besetzt.`
- `Günstigeres Pferd gesucht? Ideen des teureren Experten nutzen und hoffen, dass der Nachfolger in die Hufspuren passt.`
- `Viel Theater, wenig Klarheit: unklare Budgets, unklare Erwartungen, unverbindliche Kommunikation.`

Both-sides reform block:

Companies / institutions should provide:

- clear budgets and real hiring / decision intent
- transparent processes with realistic timelines
- respect for applicant time and expertise
- case studies only with clear limits, compensation or defined non-use boundaries
- honest decisions and feedback

Applicants / employees should provide:

- reliability and performance readiness
- honest handling of tasks, deadlines and mistakes
- loyalty and protection of legitimate organizational interests
- no misuse of trust or responsibility
- accountability for their own actions and consequences

Governance example:

`More truth in employment references - clearly regulated.`

Only for grave, documented, legally reviewed cases with safeguards against misuse, e.g. major safety breaches, severe violations of work instructions with risk to third parties, breaches of trade secrets, or significant damage to organizations / customers / society.

## Acceptance Criteria

A future Voxy/eDebatte output follows this default template when:

- Voxy acts as host and structures the debate.
- The leading question is visible and public-interest oriented.
- The output contains a thesis, observations, both-sides obligations and a reform/governance block.
- The style uses the dark VOG/eDebatte neon-blue studio visual language.
- The copy is pointed but not personally accusatory.
- The output remains review-gated, dossier-bound and publication-safe.

A future Voxy/eDebatte co-creation flow follows this default when:

- the author's raw intent is captured before drafting
- the topic is reframed from personal experience into a structural debate question
- facts, assumptions and open points are separated
- the author confirms the direction before final output rendering
- editorial review remains mandatory before publication
