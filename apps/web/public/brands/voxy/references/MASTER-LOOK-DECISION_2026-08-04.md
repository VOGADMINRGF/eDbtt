# Voxy Master-Look Decision

Status: human-approved visual direction, 2026-08-04.

## Canonical character

Voxy is one canonical digital moderator character. eDebatte and VoiceOpenGov member experiences use theme variants of the same character, rig, proportions and motion system; they are not separate figures.

## Theme variants

### eDebatte

- deep navy / blue environment
- electric-blue primary signal
- restrained turquoise only where needed for system continuity
- blue broadcast waveform
- eDebatte wordmark/pocket overlay remains crisp and replaceable

### VoiceOpenGov member

- deep navy base
- turquoise or turquoise-to-electric-blue primary signal
- member treatment may use a controlled gradient
- VOG lapel pin remains crisp and replaceable
- no separate anatomy, face or rig

## Invariants

- exactly five fingers for every approved visible hand pose
- no generative hand improvisation at render time
- stable head, eye, lid, brow, arm and hand layer IDs
- VOG pin and eDebatte pocket mark are independent approved overlays
- one waveform behind Voxy; never through logo or editable text zones
- 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 share the same canonical timeline and safe-area contract
- no lip-sync requirement
- no baked dynamic typography in production character assets

## Outfit variations

Outfit variations are allowed only when they preserve:

- character proportions and head silhouette
- headphones and facial identity
- approved hand anatomy
- layer IDs and pivot points
- brand overlay anchors
- crop safety for all canonical formats

## Reference-board status

The approved visual boards supplied in the product review are visual target references only. They are not production typography, layered masters, legal-rights evidence or anatomy source files. Small generated labels must not be copied as final text. Production text and marks are rendered from native editable UI/vector assets.

## Remaining manual gates

- provenance and unrestricted marketing-use rights
- 200-percent visual inspection of hands, face, transparent edges, VOG pin and eDebatte pocket mark
- real 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 crop review
- final layered master and pivot acceptance
